# V0.21 implementation progress

V0.20 was committed as `01eb74d` before this pass. This is a progress record,
not a claim that the V0.21 or V0.22 definitions of done have been met.

## Team authority foundation

- Separate member, role, assignment, team and delegation identifiers.
- Five built-in roles and custom roles with explicit allowed/prohibited actions.
- Bounded teams (32 members/roles, 128 assignments) with unique authenticated sessions.
- Scoped, expiring assignments; authority intersects source, target role and team ceiling.
- Delegations are immutable, bounded to two links, and recheck parent expiry and revocation.
- Team revisions invalidate prior delegations. Unknown scope containment fails closed.
- Runtime resolves local identity and grants, discarding caller-supplied assessments.
- Publication rechecks team authority; historical decisions retain their recorded context.
- Registered team sessions cannot omit their team context to bypass role checks.
- Existing concrete capability and external effect authorization remain required.
- Schema 26 stores team revisions, delegations, revocations and append-only events.
- Local administrative CLI: team list/import/show/inspect/roles/authority/history;
  delegation list/import/show/validate/revoke. No agent self-assignment endpoint.

## Review and evidence layer

Schema 27 adds immutable review declarations, contributions, findings and explicit
local-user dispositions. Reviews bind exact actions and plan/composition revisions.
High-risk execution requires distinct proposer/reviewer/executor members and
evidence diversity. Canonical path deduplication, known contradictions, common-mode
dependencies, evidence freshness and publication-time rechecks are implemented.
Explicit gates cannot be skipped by omitting a review ID. See `team-review.md` for
the API, CLI, trust boundary and limitations. This is still a partial V0.21 pass.

Review-layer validation: the full serial all-target/all-feature suite passed
409 tests with zero failures and five ignored. After the final clock guard, the
focused team and review suites passed all 29 tests (including 17 review tests).
The CLI help smoke check passed. The review fixtures use canonical local evidence
records; they are not a new controlled multi-agent benchmark.
Final review-layer formatting and all-target/all-feature Clippy checks passed.

## Handoffs and plan responsibility

Schema 28 stores immutable, hashed, reference-only handoffs. Sender and recipient
assignments, authenticated identities, scope, revision and expiry are checked.
Sensitive/restricted data delivery is rejected pending a dedicated adapter.
Optional role/member bindings now constrain plan steps; delegations can pin a plan
revision and children cannot drop that restriction. Step completion rechecks live
team authority and review state. See `team-handoffs.md` for exact boundaries.

Remaining work still includes blind knowledge exposure, challenge/formation planning,
effect receipt actor provenance, recovery-specific handoffs, composition role binding,
economics/curriculum/assurance integration and the complete V0.21 benchmark/report.

Handoff-layer validation: all 88 tests passed across the learning, plan, substrate,
team and team-review suites. These include recipient identity/scope checks,
reference preservation, rejected sensitive payloads, plan responsibility, pinned
delegation revisions and authority changes before step completion. Formatting,
all-target/all-feature Clippy with warnings denied, handoff CLI help and whitespace
checks passed. The full suite result above applies to the preceding review layer.
