# V0.21 implementation status

V0.21 is complete. It extends the V0.20 plan-validity foundation with bounded
multi-agent roles, delegation, evidence diversity, challenge assignments,
responsibility ownership, recovery handoffs, effect actor provenance, assurance,
economics, curriculum integration, and a deterministic comparative benchmark.

## Delivered controls

- Separate member, role, assignment, team, delegation, responsibility, challenge,
  reassignment, and recovery-handoff identities.
- Five built-in roles plus custom roles with explicit responsibilities, capability
  envelopes, prohibited actions, and role-specific knowledge policies.
- Bounded, revisioned teams whose authenticated assignments and delegated authority
  are rechecked at decision publication and effect commit.
- Immutable review, contribution, finding, disposition, handoff, governance, team
  record, and effect actor histories across schemas 26 through 29.
- Evidence-based team fusion that deduplicates canonical paths, exposes common-mode
  dependencies, and ignores votes and agent counts.
- Full, role-scoped, blind-challenge, and minimal knowledge delivery with recorded
  views and post-assignment challenge-yield checks.
- One-owner responsibility records for plans, plan steps, composition steps, claims,
  experiments, effects, recovery, and reviews.
- Explicit recovery handoffs that bind validated failure evidence, plan state, and
  receipts for committed effects without granting Executor capabilities.
- Formation and economics recommendations that can request zero additional runs
  when the existing evidence is sufficiently diverse.
- Scoped team assurance profiles, bounded team curriculum goals, structured bridge
  DTOs, local administrative CLI commands, and non-promoting guard recommendations.

The trust boundaries, limitations, and requirement-by-requirement accounting are
in `team-governance.md`, `team-review.md`, `team-handoffs.md`, and
`v0.21-report.md`. V0.22 distributed synchronization remains a separate pass.

not a production performance guarantee.
